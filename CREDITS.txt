CREDITS
=======

Desktop Cat draws its character from third-party pixel art.
Full attribution follows, as required by the applicable licences.

The same credit is reachable from inside the app: right-click the tray icon
and choose "Stats & credits".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY CHARACTER  --  "Cat Fighter Sprite Sheet"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Artist : dogchicken
  Source : https://opengameart.org/content/cat-fighter-sprite-sheet
  Licence: CC-BY 3.0  ( https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ )

  The sprite sheet is redistributed here UNMODIFIED. Frames are decoded at
  runtime and their opaque blue canvas is keyed out; the art itself is not
  edited.

  Frame size 50x50. After the background is punched out, each animation has
  its own content box, and every clip is drawn at an integer zoom with
  nearest-neighbour filtering so the pixels stay crisp and never shimmer.

  ALL 12 CLIPS ARE USED. They were assigned to moods by measured motion
  energy (changed pixels per second) and silhouette spread, not by eye:

    clip        file            fr  motion  spread   used for
    ----------  --------------  --  ------  ------   ------------------------
    play        cat_a5.gif      24   2988    1.39    Play, Game
    surprise    cat_a8.gif       8   2175    1.28    Surprise
    sad         cat_a6.gif       8   2105    1.22    Sad
    talk        cat_a9.gif       6   1780    1.28    Talking
    dance       cat_a3.gif      14   1388    1.22    Dancing
    stretch     cat_a4.gif      18   1362    1.06    Stretch
    cheer       cat_a7.gif      14   1205    1.44    Cheer (petting combo)
    remind      cat_a2.gif      12    976    1.39    Reminder
    pet         cat_a1.gif      19    716    1.17    Happy (being petted)
    idle        cat_idle.gif     4   1425    1.00    Idle
    walk        cat_walk.gif     4   1352    1.00    Walking
    jump        cat_jump.gif    14   1451    1.00    Being carried

  A note on one of them, measured frame by frame:

    cat_a1.gif is the least animated clip in the pack -- fewer changed pixels
    per second than simply standing still. It therefore backs only the gentle
    "being petted" wiggle, and every louder reaction got a clip of its own.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROVENANCE AND THIRD-PARTY CHARACTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The character drawn here is an original-style pixel-art cat. No asset,
  model, or runtime belonging to any third-party character or franchise is
  included in or required by this application. No Live2D or Cubism Core
  runtime is used: every animation is a plain GIF decoded at runtime.

  Nothing here requires network access. The application contains no
  networking code of any kind, and collects no telemetry.
